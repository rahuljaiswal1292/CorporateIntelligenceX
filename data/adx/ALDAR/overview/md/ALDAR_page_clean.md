## COMPANY OVERVIEW

Aldar Properties PJSC headquartered in Abu Dhabi, UAE, is a leading real estate development, management, and investment company. We specialize in creating vibrant ecosystems built around a deep understanding of our stakeholders’ needs.
Our thoughtfully designed projects foster thriving communities where families can grow, corporate cultures can innovate, and educational institutions can excel. We bring together talented individuals, businesses, and partners within a dynamic ecosystem, founded on robust relationships and a commitment to excellence.
At Aldar, we are driven by a human-centric approach, prioritizing governance, ethics, inclusivity, and sustainability. Our goal is to generate a positive impact across all our endeavours, setting the standard for responsible and forward-thinking development.
