## COMPANY OVERVIEW

Established in 1981, the Group is one of the leading digital transformation and system integration services providers in the United Arab Emirates. The Group also has smaller operations in KSA (launched in 2022) and Qatar (re-launched in 2022), which the Group plans to grow in the future.
The Group develops and builds customised electronic (computer-based) solutions for customers (including businesses and governmental bodies) principally by using third party-developed hardware, software, networking and storage products (developed by many of the world's leading technology companies). In doing so, the Group typically integrates numerous products and/or services into a solution and/or service delivered by the Group. The Group also recruits and outsources specialist IT personnel to customers.
