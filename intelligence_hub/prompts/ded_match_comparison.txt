1. **Business Description/Activities Match** (40%)
   - Does the DED license activities match the company's industry/description?
   - Are the business sectors aligned (e.g., trading, construction, finance)?

2. **Company Name Similarity** (30%)
   - How close is the DED trade name to the canonical company name?
   - Consider English/Arabic variations, abbreviations, legal suffixes (LLC, PJSC, etc.)

3. **Location/Headquarters Match** (20%)
   - Is the company's headquarters location consistent with DED registration?
   - UAE companies registered in DED should have UAE headquarters

4. **Other Details** (10%)
   - Partner/shareholder names match with known executives (CEO, founders)
   - License issue dates align with company founding year
   - License categories match the industry sector

- DED activities are often generic (e.g., "GENERAL TRADING") - match the spirit, not exact words
- Large companies may have multiple licenses with different activities
- Trade names may differ from marketing names (e.g., "EMAAR PROPERTIES PJSC" vs "Emaar Properties")
- If SERP data is limited, prioritize name similarity over other factors
- If no clear winner, select the candidate with highest name similarity score

Output format:

  }
}
